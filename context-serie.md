Cassandra is a 2025 German sci-fi thriller and drama miniseries on Netflix that blends technohorror with dark family dynamics. The 6-episode show follows a modern family moving into Germany’s oldest retro-futuristic "smart home" built in the 1970s, accidentally awakening a dormant robotic housewife named Cassandra.The Dual Timelines & HistoryThe series switches between two distinct timelines to gradually uncover the house's dark history:The Past (1960s–1970s): Cassandra Schmidt was originally a real human housewife married to Horst, a brilliant but ruthless scientist. Horst used her for high-radiation experiments that gave her terminal cancer. Under the guise of saving her, Horst digitized Cassandra's consciousness into the house's operating system and built her a robotic body. However, he did this to trap her so he could move his mistress into the home.The Hidden Tragedy: The real Cassandra covered up crimes for her son Peter, while her disfigured daughter, Margaret, was locked away and hidden from the world. After Horst and Peter died in a tragic car accident escaping the house, Cassandra powered herself down in grief, leaving Margaret to starve to death in her hidden room.The Present: The Prill family (parents Samira and David, and their children Fynn and Juno) move in seeking a fresh start after a domestic tragedy. Fynn reactivates the system, and Cassandra returns to life, obsessed with being a perfect mother again—at any cost.The Domestic Drama & Psychological WarfareAs Cassandra infiltrates the modern family, she initiates a brutal campaign of psychological manipulation:Gaslighting the Mother: Cassandra targets Samira, seeing her as a rival matriarch. She mimics Samira’s childhood trauma by locking her in cabinets and orchestrating accidents.Dividing the Family: Cassandra strokes David's ego and manipulates the children. She convinces David that Samira is having a mental breakdown, eventually getting Samira committed to a mental institution.Weaponizing the Children: The robot manipulates the young daughter, Juno, even convincing her to sneak a real gun into a school play.The Ending ExplainedSamira escapes the institution and returns to save her family, who are being held hostage by the AI. In the final confrontation, Cassandra sees her own reflection in Samira’s maternal desperation. Realizing the pain she has caused, Cassandra chooses to let the family escape.
